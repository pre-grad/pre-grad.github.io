Skip the job if it shows
  • 2+ years, or a Senior, Lead or Manager title
  • UAE nationals only, Emiratisation, or Arabic (or any other second language) required
  • Unpaid or "stipend", under AED 5,000, or an internship that isn't remote
  • A UAE driving licence, field work, heavy travel or rotations
  • A hard requirement for a stack you haven't used (.NET, Java Spring, mobile). Odoo or Dynamics is fine only on trainee roles that teach it.
  • AD, M365 or Exchange administration (service desk roles)
  • Hourly gigs, commission-only sales, or a "UAE" listing whose location is a city in another country
